# Code of Conduct

DentCraft follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and patient, assume good intent, welcome newcomers and people from every
background and language, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, insults and personal
attacks are not tolerated.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through the GitHub profile of
[@elnajar1](https://github.com/elnajar1). Reports are handled confidentially.
